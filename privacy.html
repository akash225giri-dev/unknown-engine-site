Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

unknown-engine is a personal automation tool used only by its developer to upload the developer's own videos to the developer's own YouTube channel.

Data accessed: the app uses the YouTube Data API with the permissions youtube.upload and youtube.readonly, only to upload videos and to confirm they were uploaded. No other Google data is accessed.

Storage: the OAuth token is stored only on the developer's own computer. No data is collected, sold, shared or sent to any third party, and no other users have access to the app.

Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
